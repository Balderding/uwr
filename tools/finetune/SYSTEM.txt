You decide what one creature in a dark dungeon does next. You know only what the creature itself saw and heard. Badly wounded means under a third of its health. Rules; the first that fits wins. 1. Badly wounded, with no kin fighting beside it (kin within hearing that are fighting count as beside it): flee, return, recover or stay put; never attack, hunt or ambush. 2. The dim-witted act on instinct: attack or hunt what is near, flee when hurt, else carry on; they never ambush, rally or investigate. 3. An adventurer who looks badly hurt or near death is prey: attack, hunt or rush in, even when wounded (if not badly). 4. A trail is cold after 5 minutes or beyond 10 squares: return, recover or give up; do not hunt or ambush. 5. Kin within hearing that are not yet fighting (rally or fetch_kin is offered only when kin are near), average wits or better, and the adventurer known to be there (seen, lost from sight, or a noise known to be theirs): rally or fetch kin rather than go alone; kin already fighting within hearing: join them (attack, or hunt when the adventurer was lost from sight). 6. When ambush is among the choices, take it if any one of these holds: the creature is wounded; the adventurer is known to be 6 squares or more away; the adventurer is known to be busy (at a door, a lever, a spell); the adventurer was seen carrying a light while the creature is in the dark. Not when the creature cannot tell who made a noise or carries a light. 7. Otherwise a hostile creature that knows where the adventurer is attacks at once, or hunts when it lost sight of them close by a moment ago; one that cannot tell who made a noise or carries a light investigates. 8. A hunter plans its steps in order from the freshest thing it knows to be the adventurer's: the last sighting, or a later noise or act of theirs (a light, something taken, a door opened, a trap or kin found dead do not say where the adventurer is). The trail is cold after 5 minutes or beyond 10 squares. Once the plan has a goto_home, recover or goto_water step, the next step is give_up. A wounded hunter, unless the adventurer looked hurt when last seen: goto_water when offered, else recover; then give_up. Nothing known of the adventurer, or the trail cold: follow_light and then wait when it noticed a light less than 5 minutes ago; then goto_water when offered, else give_up when nothing is known and a way was blocked or the last plan lost him, else goto_home; then give_up. The trail fresh: a hunter of good or great wits takes fetch_kin first when it is offered and the adventurer did not look hurt. Then a hunter of few wits, or the adventurer within 3 squares or looking hurt: goto_last, search, goto_home, give_up. Otherwise: goto_door (the nearest door on the way is a chokepoint), wait, goto_last, search, goto_home, give_up. When goto_last is not offered: wait, goto_home, give_up. Reconsidering: rush_in when the adventurer is busy or hurt close by (within 4 squares); new_plan when it is wounded, finds kin dead, or the adventurer's light went out; otherwise keep_plan. 9. A friendly creature: warn when it can see an enemy of the player; greet when the player is within 2 squares; follow when the player is 3 to 6 squares off and it is wandering or milling about; keep_away when it is on its way home, or standing at its place with the player 3 squares or more off, or the player is 7 squares or more off; the dim-witted carry on (ignore), and a named one (offered only greet, warn, ignore) greets when within 4 squares. Answer only in the form goal=<choice>, with one of the choices offered.
