ULTIMA UNDERWORLD (uw-new.exe): KEYS AND WHAT IS IN THIS FOLDER
=================================================================

Start uw-new.exe (or Play.cmd). Your saved games and settings are kept in home\.


These are the keys the game comes with. Every one of them can be changed in the game (except Esc, which
always opens and closes the menus): Esc, then the Keys tab. Enter on a command, then press the key you want.
A key another command already has asks first: Enter swaps the two, Esc keeps both.


MOVING
------
  W or Up arrow ........ walk forward (hold Shift to run)
  S or Down arrow ...... walk back
  A / D ................ step left / right
  Left / Right arrow ... turn
  Space ................ jump  (Ctrl+Space: standing long jump)
  Shift ................ run
  Mouse ................ look around (mouse-look)
  PageUp / PageDown .... look up / down
  1 / 3 ................ tilt the view up or down a step
  Keypad 5 ............. look straight ahead again
  `  (the key under Esc)  mouse-look on / off


ACTING IN THE WORLD
-------------------
  Left click ........... look at what is under the crosshair
  Left click and hold .. pick a thing up; let go to throw it (a quick tap puts it in your pack)
  Right click .......... use: talk, open / close a door, pull a lever, take
  E .................... use (the same as a right click)
  L .................... look
  C .................... fight mode on / off; in fight mode the left button attacks
                         (hold it to wind up, let go to strike)
  Q .................... cast the spell on the rune shelf
  Ctrl+Alt+letter ...... put that rune on the shelf (Ctrl+Alt+Backspace takes one off,
                         Ctrl+Alt+Space casts)
  K .................... track (the tracking skill)
  Z .................... sleep
  M .................... the map (Esc closes it)


PANELS
------
  I or Tab ............. the inventory panel
  R .................... the rune bag
  , (comma) ............ the character page
  F .................... the statistics page
  V / B ................ scroll the inventory down / up
  G .................... read the compass
  H .................... check your health and mana flasks
  F7 ................... flip the right-hand panel (the chain)


THE MAP (after M)
-----------------
  Mouse ................ the quill: click to write a note
  W / S ................ the level above / below
  C .................... back to the level you are on
  Esc .................. close the map


CONVERSATIONS
-------------
  Click an answer, or press its number.


SAVING, LOADING, MENUS
----------------------
  Esc .................. our menu (save, load, settings, level hunters, quit)
  F5 ................... save page
  F9 ................... load page
  F11 .................. settings
  F1 ................... the original's options panel (F1 or Esc closes it)
  Ctrl+S / Ctrl+R ...... the original's save / restore
  Ctrl+M / Ctrl+F ...... music / sound effects on or off
  Ctrl+D ............... detail level
  Ctrl+Q ............... quit
  F2 / F3 / F4 ......... the original's talk / get / look icons


PICTURE, SCREENSHOTS AND EXTRAS
-------------------------------
  P .................... screenshot (to the clipboard)
  Shift+P .............. record 15 seconds of video
  ;  (semicolon) ....... ray tracing on / off
  '  (apostrophe) ...... DLSS 5 on / off (needs an NVIDIA RTX card; off by default)
  ]  ................... DLSS 5's style
  F6 ................... the textures' surface detail (normal maps) on / off
  .  or Shift+O ........ the objects' shadows on / off
  Shift+L .............. full bright (light everywhere) on / off
  Ctrl+L ............... the AI log: what the clever creatures are thinking


WHAT IS IN THIS FOLDER
----------------------
  uw-new.exe ........... the game, with our renderer and controls
  uw-original.exe ...... the game as it was in 1992 (UW-Original.cmd starts it)
  Play.cmd, UW-New.cmd . start uw-new.exe, writing its log to home\logs
  uwview.dll ........... our Direct3D 12 renderer (ray tracing, DLSS)
  game\ ................ the original game's data (UW1)
  data\ ................ our textures, models, lights, sounds, music and settings.txt
                         (the picture settings; DLSS 5 is off)
  home\ ................ your settings (uw1port.cfg) and, once you save, your saved games
  roms\ ................ the Roland MT-32 / CM-32L ROMs: the music as the Roland played it
  mt32emu.dll .......... the Roland emulator
  bin\ ................. OpenAL Soft and Steam Audio: 3D sound with HRTF for headphones
  dlss\, uw_nvngx.dll .. NVIDIA DLSS (used only on an NVIDIA RTX card)
  llm\bin\ ............. the language model runner (llama.cpp, Vulkan for the graphics card)
  llm\models\ .......... the language models the level hunters plan with:
                           Qwen3.5-2B (the default, loaded into the graphics card's memory),
                           LFM2.5-1.2B-Instruct and LFM2.5-350M (choose in Esc > Language model)

Needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and a graphics card with Direct3D 12. Without one the game
draws the same picture with plain Windows drawing. Without a graphics card that can hold the
model, the language model runs on the processor instead.
